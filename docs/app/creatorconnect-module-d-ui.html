Scope & Architecture

What this module covers: Complete visual design and screen architecture for Home & Dashboard (D01–D04), Discovery & Search (D05–D08, D14), Public Profile Details (D09–D11), and Recommendations & Matching (D12–D13, D15–D16).

SOW Contract Reconciliation: Built directly against SOW §9 (Discovery & Search), SOW §10 (Portfolio Showcase), SOW §13 (Rule-Based Matchmaking), SOW §20 (Notification System), and SOW §22 (Web & Mobile Dashboard Parity). In accordance with SOW §13, recommendations use predefined business rules (category, city, rating, follower range), explicitly excluding unsupported AI engines from this phase.

Role-Aware System: The shell is shared across all four user roles (Creator/Influencer, Production Professional, Brand/Company, Podcaster), with content blocks dynamically populated based on active role permissions.

How Matching Works

CreatorConnect uses deterministic business rules to connect relevant creators and brands:

1
Category & Skill Overlap
We pair creators and briefs sharing primary taxonomy and specific deliverable tags.
2
Geographic Proximity
Profiles within your city and surrounding regional hub are prioritized for in-person shoots.
3
Verification & Reviews
Verified accounts with 4.5+ star ratings receive higher algorithmic weight.
SOW §13 Note: AI-generated semantic matching is explicitly excluded from this phase and will be offered in a subsequent release.

Navigation Flow Architecture

GoRouter routing paths & deep linking structure
GoRouter Route Tree (Module D):
/home (Tab 0) ──> /notifications (Modal slide-up)
/discover (Tab 1)
  ├── /discover/search?q={query}&category={cat}&city={city} (Push)
  │    ├── /discover/filters (Bottom Sheet modal)
  │    └── /discover/sort (Bottom Sheet modal)
  ├── /discover/recommendations (Push dedicated feed)
  │    └── /discover/recommendations/explainer (Bottom Sheet)
  └── /profile/:userId (Shared Public Profile view)
      ├── /profile/:userId/options (Bottom Sheet: Report/Block)
      └── /chat/:userId (Direct handoff to Module E Chat Thread)

Open Decisions Before Flutter Build

1. Connect vs. Follow Semantics: The UI implements mutual "Connect" for creator-to-creator and one-way "Follow" for brand profiles. Confirm whether connection requires explicit acceptance in backend messaging (SOW §14).

2. Regional City Hubs: Screen D07 includes an "Include nearby regional hub" toggle (e.g. Noida/Gurugram included in Delhi NCR). Confirm whether the PostgreSQL database stores latitude/longitude coordinates or uses enum city clusters.

3. Quota Limits on Free Plan: Screen POP-D02 enforces a 5-connection daily ceiling on Free accounts before prompting for Creator Pro subscription (SOW §17). Confirm initial limit thresholds with client.
