01

Purpose, Open-Source Benchmarks & Architecture

Rules R-APP · R-INV · Fastify · Prisma · Riverpod
Core Business Purpose of Module H:
Module H governs two-way recruitment liquidity on CreatorConnect:
  1. Inbound Applications (H01–H03): How creators, production professionals, and podcasters discover assignment briefs, evaluate budget parity, submit concise tailored pitches with portfolio proof, and monitor their status through review, shortlisting, and hiring.
  2. Direct Outbound Invites (H04–H07): How brands, creators, or podcasters proactively headhunt verified talent with a private targeted brief, bypassing public marketplace noise.
Open-Source & Industry UI Benchmark Analysis:
  • Contra & Dribbble Freelance: Eliminates bloated traditional 5-page cover letters in favor of a clean, structured pitch form (50–1,000 characters) coupled with 3 verified media showcase items. Live take-home payout calculations (Client Quote − 0% Platform Fee = Net Creator Payout) increase conversion and clarity.
  • Upwork & Wellfound (AngelList): Clear quota meters preventing spam ("3 of 5 monthly applications remaining", reset on 1st). Time-decayed invitations with clear expiration indicators ("Expires in 5 days") drive urgency and rapid response times.
  • Figma / Linear State Machines: Transparent linear status tracker (`Submitted` → `Under Review` → `Shortlisted` → `Accepted & Funded`) with real-time feedback and direct in-chat handoff.

Contract / DTO Attributes Carried in UI State Validation & Bounds Target Screens
